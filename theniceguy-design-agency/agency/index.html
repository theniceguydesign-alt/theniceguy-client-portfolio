The Process

How It Works

01

Kickoff & Deposit

We hop on a 30-min call to discuss your goals, review your content, and lock in the project scope. A 50% deposit gets us started immediately.

02

Design & Build

I design and build your site from scratch — every section, every animation, every detail crafted to convert your visitors into customers.

03

Revisions

You review the live preview and send feedback. I make up to 2 rounds of revisions to make sure everything is exactly how you want it.

04

Launch

After your final approval and the remaining 50%, your site goes live. I handle domain setup, hosting, and make sure everything runs perfectly.
